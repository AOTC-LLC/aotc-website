Questions

Running in another city?

01Can AOTC run a mobile billboard campaign in several cities at once?

Yes. A multi-market campaign can run the same creative on the same day in several cities, each on a route planned around its own audience and venues, or move from city to city as a tour. You get one plan, one point of contact and photos from every market.

02Is it the same team in every market?

Yes. Every route is planned by the AOTC team in Chicago, with the same brief, the same creative standards and the same photo record after every run, whichever city it runs in.

03My city isn't listed. Can you still run there?

Usually, yes. The market pages cover 49 metros, and AOTC plans campaigns across the lower 48. Text (773) 708-1400 with the city and the date and we'll tell you what's possible.

04How far ahead should I book outside Chicago?

As early as you can, and earlier still for big event weeks like CES, SXSW, Art Basel or a Super Bowl city, when dates fill quickly. Short-notice campaigns are sometimes possible, so text (773) 708-1400 and ask.

05Do the rules for mobile billboards change from city to city?

Yes. Some cities regulate advertising vehicles, and venues set their own rules on their property. AOTC checks the current rules for your format and route in every market before it quotes.
