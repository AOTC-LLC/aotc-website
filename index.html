Questions

Straight answers.

01What is a mobile billboard truck?

A truck with built-in digital screens that drives your ad along a route you choose. The screens on both sides and the back carry the full ad (AOTC's own truck also has a front screen); it plays stills or full-motion video, and runs on GPS-tracked routes.

02Who offers mobile digital billboards nationwide?

AOTC, LLC (Ads of the Century) plans and runs mobile digital billboard truck campaigns across the U.S. from its home base in Chicago: single-city routes, same-day launches in several markets, tours and trade-show circuits. See all markets.

03How much does a mobile billboard cost?

Published industry rate guides put LED billboard trucks at roughly $1,200 to $3,600 per truck per day, usually with a minimum of about four hours. AOTC quotes include route planning and creative, usually the same day. See every cost question in the FAQ.

04Do I share the screen with other advertisers?

Yes, on a standard booking: AOTC specializes in rotational campaigns. Up to six brands take 10-second turns, never two from the same category, which keeps the price down. Want the truck to yourself? Book it exclusively. Your quote always says which one you're getting.

05How fast can AOTC book a campaign?

Quotes usually come back the same day, and AOTC has been on site in under 24 hours for a real deadline. Text (773) 708-1400 with the city and the date.
