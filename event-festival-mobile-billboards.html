Questions

Planning around an event?

01Can a mobile billboard target a specific event or festival?

Yes. AOTC plans routes around the event itself: the streets attendees use to get there, the entrances and perimeter, and the surrounding blocks, timed to when the crowd arrives and leaves. Belmont Cragin Vintage Fest used a neighborhood route followed by the truck at the entrance.

02Do I need a booth or sponsorship to advertise at a festival?

No. The truck runs on public streets around the event, so you can reach the festival crowd without a booth. Sponsors and exhibitors also use a truck to drive people to their booth.

03Is my brand the only one on the truck?

Not on a standard booking. AOTC specializes in rotational campaigns: up to six brands take 10-second turns on the screens, never two from the same category. The Festival of the Lakes routes in Hammond, for example, ran three brands at a one-third share of voice each. If you want the truck to yourself for the event, ask for an exclusive booking; your quote states which you're getting.

04Can you cover festivals, conventions and trade shows outside Chicago?

Yes. AOTC plans and runs event campaigns nationwide, including tours and trade-show circuits that move city to city, with one plan and one point of contact. Rules for mobile billboards vary by city, so AOTC checks them for every market before quoting.

05Can we put a QR code on the truck for tickets or donations?

Yes. A QR code or short link on the screens is the simplest way to count response at an event, whether it's ticket sales, donations, sign-ups or a visit to your booth.

06Is the truck available for private events?

Yes. The LED screens also work as a mobile event screen for festivals, corporate events, watch parties, movie nights, birthday parties and memorials.
