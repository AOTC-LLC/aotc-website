What does a mobile LED billboard do?

It puts your ad in front of people at the moment they're there: commuters at 8, the lunch crowd at noon, fans at game time, the nightlife strip after 10. AOTC, LLC specializes in rotational campaigns, sharing the screens in 10-second turns with a few other brands and never a competitor, and books the truck exclusively when you want it to yourself.

Questions

Mobile LED basics.

01What is a mobile LED billboard?

A truck with full-size LED screens that drives your ad to the audience: the business district at rush hour, the ballpark on game day, the festival gates, the neighborhood around your store. AOTC, LLC plans the route by time of day, designs the screens and sends a GPS-tracked route and photos after every run.

02What's the difference between a rotational and an exclusive booking?

A rotational booking shares the truck's screens with up to five other brands in 10-second turns, never a competitor, which keeps the price down; it's what AOTC specializes in. An exclusive booking takes the truck off the market for your run, with only your brand on every screen.

03How many screens does the truck have?

The full ad runs on both sides and the rear: each side is 11.76 × 6.30 ft and the rear 6.71 × 6.30 ft, so it reads from both sidewalks and the traffic behind. AOTC's own truck also has a front screen above the cab.

04What does every booking include?

A route planned by daypart around your audience, still-image creative designed in-house, a rotation with no competing brand, Golden Ticket access to watch the truck live, and a GPS-tracked route with photos of your ad on the street after every run.

05How do I book?
