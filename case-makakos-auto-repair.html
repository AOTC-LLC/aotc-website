Makako's Auto Repair × AOTC

Makako's partnered with AOTC to turn a neighborhood auto shop into a festival-day brand people could see, remember, and respond to. The win wasn't immediate foot traffic — it was repeated visibility in front of a large audience, with bold design and a direct-response message impossible to miss.

Hammond, IN · Festival RouteJuly 18, 2026Automotive · Local Service

The Case Study

A local shop with a big visibility objective

The Opportunity

Thousands were already gathering around Hammond. The move wasn't to chase them into the shop — it was to make sure the brand was seen while attention was high.

The Strategy

A brand-exposure route with memorable visuals and a no-friction CTA. The design did the heavy lifting: bold logo, high contrast, and a prompt that told people exactly what to do.

What AOTC Did

A six-hour Hammond route mixing static and motion creative kept the message simple on purpose: We Diagnose. Call Now. Get Answers.

Why It Worked

Audience first. Simple CTA. Exposure over foot traffic.

The route met people where they already were — inside a high-traffic festival window instead of waiting for them to discover the shop later. "Call Now. Get Answers." made the message immediate and practical, especially for drivers who value fast help. The goal was recognition and recall — success came from being seen by a large audience, not from overstating walk-ins.

The Creative

Bold identity. Clear offer. Zero guesswork.

The creative said exactly what needed to be said, fast. Black, gold, white, and red created immediate visual punch and made the message easy to read at a glance. No over-explaining — a clean problem-solution message that helped a neighborhood mechanic speak to a much larger audience without losing the directness that makes local service businesses work.
