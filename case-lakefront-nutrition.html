The Case Study

When the crowd is nearby — but not at your door

The Opportunity

Lakefront had timely summer specials to promote, but the Whiting storefront wasn't close enough to the festival footprint for foot traffic alone to tell the story.

The Real Objective

Brand exposure and recognition — not an unsupported claim of store visits. Success meant being seen by the large Hammond audience and creating a display people wanted to photograph.

What AOTC Did

A focused six-hour Hammond route, rotating Lakefront's identity, Sunshine IV special, and motion product creative — delivering 33% share of voice with organic photo interaction.

The Exposure Strategy

Seen. Remembered. Shared.

Because the store sat outside the immediate festival footprint, AOTC treated visibility as a sequence. Go to the audience — carry the message into Hammond while tens of thousands moved through the market. Make the offer memorable — bright seasonal creative paired the identity with a specific Sunshine IV message, not a generic logo. Earn a second look — people photographed the truck, creating organic social interaction that extended value beyond a standard drive-by.

The Creative

Brand first. Product second. Motion throughout.

The display balanced immediate brand recognition with a timely festival offer, while motion kept the campaign visually active throughout the route — name, contact, and social identity alongside the Sunshine IV special positioned around hydration, energy, and recovery.
