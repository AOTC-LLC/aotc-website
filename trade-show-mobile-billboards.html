How do you advertise at a trade show with a mobile billboard?

An LED billboard truck loops the public streets around the convention center, the official hotels and the routes attendees walk, timed to doors opening, lunch and the end of the show day. It reaches the same buyers as the show floor, with or without a booth, and can point them to your booth number, a demo or an after-party.

Creative

What to put on the screens.

Show attendees read a truck the way they read a booth: in three seconds, walking. Give them one thing to do.

Booth number + one reason

"Booth 4417: see it live." The number big enough to read across the street, the reason short enough to remember.

A QR code to book

A meeting, a demo slot, a dinner seat. Count the scans by day and by daypart.

A countdown

The screens are digital: "Demo at 2 p.m." becomes "Demo in 30 minutes" without a reprint.

Questions

Show week questions.

01Do I need a booth to advertise at a trade show with a mobile billboard?

No. The truck runs on the public streets around the convention center and the hotels, so it reaches attendees whether or not you exhibit. Exhibitors use it to drive traffic to the booth; non-exhibitors use it to be part of the week anyway.

02Is a mobile billboard allowed at the convention center?

Venues set their own rules on their property, and cities regulate advertising vehicles on their streets. AOTC plans routes on public streets around the venue and checks the local rules for every show city before it quotes.

03When should the truck run during show week?

Around the show's own clock: the hotels and registration on arrival day, the walk to the hall before doors, the perimeter at lunch and the restaurant district after the floor closes. Most exhibitors focus on the first two show days.

04What should a trade-show ad on a truck say?

One thing to do: your booth number and a reason to stop, a QR code to book a meeting or demo, or the time and place of your party. Attendees read it in a few seconds while walking, so less is more.

05Can you cover several trade shows in different cities?

Yes. AOTC plans a whole show season under one plan, city by city, with the same creative system and photos from every show. See tours and roadshows.

06Which convention centers do you plan routes around?

Any of them. Market pages cover the streets around McCormick Place, the Las Vegas Convention Center, the Javits Center, the Orange County Convention Center, the Georgia World Congress Center, Moscone Center and more. See all markets.
