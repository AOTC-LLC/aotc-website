Can I rent an LED screen truck for an event?

Yes. AOTC's truck carries four HD LED screens (both sides, the back and the front) that run your video, slides, live content or a movie on site, for brand activations, festivals, corporate events, watch parties, movie nights and celebrations. At the Elevated Exotics event in New Buffalo, Michigan, the screens ran movies and video games live and turned a parking lot into a hangout.

Questions

Booking the screens.

01How much does it cost to rent an LED screen truck?

It depends on the hours, the location and the content. Tell AOTC the date, the place and what you want on the screens, and you'll get a quote, usually the same day. See the FAQ for the factors that move the price.

02Can the screens show a movie, a game or live content?

Yes. At the Elevated Exotics event in New Buffalo, the screens ran movies and video games live. Video, slides and live content all work; send what you have and AOTC will tell you honestly how it will look.

03Does the truck have sound?

Sound is available. AOTC doesn't produce audio, so the soundtrack comes with your content.

04What file sizes do the screens need?

1920 × 1029 px for the two side screens, 1080 × 1014 for the back and 1280 × 641 for the front, as JPG, PNG or MP4. Or send one file and AOTC will fit it.

05How quickly can the screen truck be booked?

Fast when it has to be: Elevated Exotics called the day before, and the truck was on site in under 24 hours. For weekends and summer dates, book as soon as you know your date.

06Can the truck advertise on the way to the event?

Yes. Combine a route and an on-site session: advertise the event on the streets around it, then park and become the screen.
