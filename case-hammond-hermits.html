Opening Day
Parade.

Hammond Hermits × AOTC

With less than a week to prepare, AOTC joined the Hammond Hermits Baseball & Softball League for opening day — and quickly became more than another vehicle in the parade. The truck caught the attention of players, families, and league directors, earned the lead position through Hammond, and was invited into the park for a special opening-ceremony placement near the outfield.

Hammond, Indiana Sunday, May 3, 2026 Parade + Opening Ceremony

The Case Study

A parade spot that grew into a featured role

The Notice

Opening day was approaching fast. AOTC confirmed its place in the parade with less than one week of preparation — little time for a traditional production runway.

The Arrival

The mobile LED truck immediately drew attention from ballplayers, families, and league leadership. Participation quickly became an opportunity to make the entire morning feel larger.

What AOTC Did

AOTC was given the privilege of leading the parade through Hammond, then received special access inside the park to support the opening ceremony near the outfield after Mayor Thomas McDermott Jr.'s ceremonial pitch.

Live Event Coverage

From the parade route to the ballfield

The activation moved with the event — first joining the procession through Hammond, then transitioning into the park for the ceremony placement near the outfield. The route captured the mobile displays in motion as AOTC helped lead the league through the neighborhood, and the field-side placement extended the activation into the opening ceremony and the fireworks moment following the ceremonial pitch.

The Displays

Creative built for opening-day energy

Bold baseball imagery, league identity, and celebratory messaging — readable in a glance by players, families, and spectators across the route and ceremony.

League Mission

Wide-format creative introduced the Hammond Hermits and clearly communicated the league's role teaching baseball and softball to players ages 3–18.

Primary Identity

The central league mark — flaming baseball and softball graphics with crossed bats — gave the truck an unmistakable Hammond Hermits presence throughout the morning.

Every Team Represented

A team-logo collage made the activation feel inclusive to players and families across the league's baseball and softball programs.

The Experience

From arrival to the outfield

The activation expanded in real time because the vehicle did more than show up — it gave the league a flexible entertainment and visual platform that could move with the event.

The Outcome

The clearest proof was the expanded invitation

From confirmed participant to event feature — the parade role grew because the league saw what AOTC could add to the entire opening-day experience. AOTC did not leave when the parade ended; the league expanded the activation on-site, positioned the truck near the outfield, and folded it into the opening ceremony alongside the fireworks moment.

Leadership Trust

The vehicle was selected to lead the parade rather than remain in the general procession.

Special Access

AOTC received permission to enter the park and occupy a placement not available to ordinary parade traffic.

Event Integration

The activation supported both the city route and the ceremony — one connected two-hour experience.
