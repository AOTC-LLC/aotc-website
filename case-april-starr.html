The Case Study

One realtor. One message stream. An entire weekend.

The Opportunity

St. Patrick's weekend turns downtown into a dense, constantly moving audience. The moment called for a visible personal-brand presence — not a static post or a single placement.

The Strategy

AOTC structured the route around the city's major attention drivers: river-dyeing crowds, parade traffic, bar crawls, nightlife, and the steady flow of downtown visitors.

What AOTC Did

For three straight days the LED screens carried April's face, phone number, services, and brokerage through Chicago at 100% share of voice — no competing advertisers in the rotation.

The Route

Downtown visibility from day into night

The campaign stayed visually consistent while the environment changed — from daytime event congestion to nighttime downtown movement. The result was repeated recognition across multiple audience moments rather than a single pass.

01

River Dyeing

Large morning crowds created a concentrated visibility window around one of the city's signature traditions.

02

Parade Traffic

Street closures and pedestrian activity increased the value of a message that could move with the changing traffic.

03

Bar Crawls & Nightlife

Evening routes kept the campaign active after the daytime celebrations, extending exposure across the full weekend.

The Creative

A face, a phone number, and a clear next move

The creative was built for fast recognition. April's portrait created immediate personal connection, the phone number dominated the message hierarchy, and the "Buy, Sell, Invest, Lease" line made the offer useful to buyers, sellers, investors, and renters — broad relevance kept simple.

Street-Facing Offer

A large action phrase, direct phone number, and four service categories made the message readable in seconds.

Personal Brand

April's portrait made the campaign human, recognizable, and tied to one clear real-estate professional — not a generic ad.

Brokerage Backing

The Realty of America identity added professional credibility without competing with April's personal brand.
