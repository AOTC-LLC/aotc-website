The Case Study

A radio brand in front of a live audience

The Notice

When tens of thousands are already moving through one place, the opportunity isn't to explain your brand from scratch — it's to be the one they see and remember.

The Situation

FAB 94.5 needed a route that matched the festival day's energy. Not a foot-traffic campaign — an awareness and visibility play keeping the station top of mind.

What AOTC Did

A six-hour Hammond route with FAB's creative displayed large on the LED screens, positioning the station in front of festival traffic as Chicago's #1 Hitz Station.

The Route

Built for visibility, not foot traffic

The route was designed around one outcome: place FAB 94.5 directly inside Hammond's highest-attention festival window and repeat the station message where music fans were already moving. Festival traffic concentrated listeners in one market — a highly relevant crowd, not a broad, unfocused audience. The creative gave viewers immediate next steps: listen live, visit the site, follow the station after the truck moved on.

The Creative

One message, read in seconds

The display was treated like a moving station billboard: oversized identity first, positioning second, and clear listening and social pathways last. Strong red, white, and black contrast made the station instantly recognizable from a distance while reinforcing the promise — Chicago music, Chicago identity, an always-on station listeners could connect with after the activation.
