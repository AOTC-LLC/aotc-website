The Case Study

Turning 4/20 into an event

The Notice

4/20 comes once a year, and Elevated Exotics needed more than a storefront banner to make the day feel like an event — with less than 24 hours to figure out how.

The Situation

AOTC landed the activation the day before service. The brief wasn't reach or brand exposure — it was pure engagement. Customers needed to stay, not just notice.

What AOTC Did

Brought its full four-screen setup and turned it into an entertainment layer — a rotating mix of movies and video games running live on-site, turning the parking lot into a hangout.

Why It Worked

Attract. Engage. Retain.

Attract — the scale and motion of four LED screens gave passing traffic a reason to look. Engage — games and movies turned the screens into participatory entertainment instead of static signage. Retain — the parking lot became a low-key hangout, giving customers a reason to remain on-site after they arrived.
