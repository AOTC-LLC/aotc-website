DJ GloBoy × AOTC · Festival of the Lakes

DJ GloBoy partnered with AOTC to extend the momentum of his social campaign into the streets of his hometown — placing his booking message in front of a 40,000-plus music audience gathered for Festival of the Lakes to see Moneybagg Yo and Kevin Gates.

Hammond, INJuly 18, 2026Music · Booking Campaign

The Case Study

Turning social momentum into street-level visibility

The Momentum

One of Northwest Indiana's rising DJs was already building attention online. The next move: turn that digital momentum into real-world recognition and more bookings.

The Audience

AOTC focused exclusively on GloBoy's hometown of Hammond, where 40,000+ gathered for headliners directly aligned with his sound.

What AOTC Did

A focused six-hour Hammond route kept GloBoy visible around city and event traffic, reinforcing the booking campaign with 33% share of voice.

Why It Worked

The right artist, market & moment

Every campaign decision reinforced the same goal: put a hometown DJ's booking message in front of a local audience already primed for his sound. Market fit — Hammond was where local familiarity turns exposure into name recognition. Audience fit — a hip-hop crowd matching the culture GloBoy performs in. Timing fit — the campaign supported two active booking windows: the rest of 2026 and the upcoming season.

The Creative

A booking message built to be remembered

The campaign paired a high-contrast DJ GloBoy identity with a full booking card that made the next action unmistakable: follow, connect, and book. Social handles and direct contact were consolidated into one readable, action-oriented asset.
