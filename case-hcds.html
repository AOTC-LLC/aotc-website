Case Study · Community Awareness

Own The
Weekend.

Holistic Counseling & Developmental Services × AOTC

HCDS used AOTC to turn a high-traffic holiday weekend into a focused visibility play. During Sueños weekend in downtown Chicago, the campaign secured 100% share of voice and stayed in front of festival-goers, downtown pedestrians, rideshare traffic, and concert-adjacent crowds with bold, bright creative built to stop attention fast.

Route: Chicago, ILMay 23–25, 2026Nonprofit · Mental Health

The Story

Why this activation worked

The Opportunity

Sueños weekend created a rare concentration of attention downtown. With festival, tourism, and nightlife peaking at once, HCDS had a chance to put its brand in front of 100,000+ people per day.

The Goal

Total visibility. HCDS wanted to stand out in a crowded public environment with a message that felt approachable, helpful, and immediate — especially for families who may need support.

What AOTC Did

Deployed the mobile LED route through Chicago for three consecutive days — a bright, unmissable street presence built around support, care, and clear next steps.

Live Route Coverage

The bright displays took over downtown Chicago

The campaign translated exactly as intended: bold orange creative, readable service messaging, and a direct phone number moving through downtown during Sueños weekend. The display stayed legible against Chicago's architecture, keeping HCDS recognizable from sidewalk and roadway — and a QR-led path gave viewers a practical next step.

Display Creative

A bright message system built to be seen fast

The package balanced warmth, clarity, and utility. One display introduced the brand, another explained the services, a third highlighted the call-in and QR path, and a fourth reinforced the category in the simplest terms. The gold-and-orange palette stood out immediately in traffic, and the phone number plus scannable QR gave the audience two easy paths to engage.
